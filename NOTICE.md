## BlueSage PlayerLimitLift

BlueSage PlayerLimitLift began in June 2026 as a community patch of **officer_balls' PlayerLimitLift** for On-Together:

- Original mod: https://thunderstore.io/c/on-together/p/officer_balls/officerballs_PlayerLimitLift/
- Original source: https://github.com/klautless/officer-balls-on-together-mods (with text channel fixes credited there to 岚风 雷 / Arashi_Lei)

The original was published without a license and hasn't been updated since early 2026. BlueSage rebuilt and maintains it so large lobbies keep working on current game builds. Parts of its structure and approach come from that original work, which remains officer_balls' own; BlueSage's changes and additions are offered under GPL-3.0.

**officer_balls:** thank you for the mod that started all of this ♥ If you'd like the original portions handled differently (credited another way, relicensed, or removed), reach us on the community Discord (https://discord.gg/JujMEwtN3q) and we'll take care of it promptly.
